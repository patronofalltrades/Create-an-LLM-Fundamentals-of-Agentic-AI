## Contrasting qualities

The metal pan began hot, but cool air made it cold.

The summer pavement felt hot while the shaded stone stayed cold.

Hot tea warmed the cup; cold water cooled another cup.

Warm bread rested on the counter after baking.

A warm coat protected the traveler from the wind.

The room felt warm when sunlight reached the floor.

The oven was hot during baking and cold after several hours.

A hot afternoon became a cold evening when the weather changed.

The storage jar began empty and became full after the harvest.

An empty fuel tank needs filling before it can become full.

The theater was empty before the doors opened and full after the audience arrived.

At sunrise the basket was empty; by noon it was full of apples.

Workers moved supplies from a full cart into an empty cabinet.

The workshop was noisy while the machines ran and quiet after shutdown.

A noisy hallway became quiet when the meeting started.

The market is noisy at midday but quiet before dawn.

The generator made the room noisy; switching it off made the room quiet.

The crowded cafe sounded noisy, while the reading room remained quiet.

The first room was bright, while the second room was dark.

The heavy crate stayed on the floor, but the light parcel was easy to lift.

A heavy suitcase required two people to carry it upstairs.

The bridge supports heavy trucks throughout the day.

Dense stone feels heavy compared with dry leaves.

The narrow path widened into a broad road.

The early delivery arrived before the late shipment.

The rough surface became smooth after careful sanding.

The wet towel dried in the warm sunlight.

The fast express passed the slow local service.

The cyclist chose a fast route across town.

The two doors opened in opposite directions.

A loud bell announced the end of the session.

The artist placed a round mirror above the table.

The late bus reached the depot after midnight.

The tall cabinet stood beside a short table.

The open window let in air until the caretaker closed it.

The soft fabric covered a hard wooden bench.

A soft blanket kept the child comfortable at night.

The soft brush removed dust without scratching the surface.

Fresh bread has a soft center beneath its crisp crust.

The designer selected a soft material for the chair cushion.
